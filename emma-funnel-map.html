Why we need to fill the gaps in this funnel

You've got plenty of leads to approach with these offers over the next 3–6 months, but once this network has been exhausted, you don't want to then suddenly be like sh*t, I didn't focus on expanding my audience and now I need to start from now. Instead, you've been warming up leads for the last 3–6 months so you have a continuous stream of leads into your business. The free offer is what ads drive to, which builds a new audience continuously, so you're never starting from zero.
